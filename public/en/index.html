What happens today

The fair closes at six. Your work ends at two in the morning.

Eighty cards, three hundred photos, a notebook even you can't read. Which booth was this photo from? What price did this supplier quote? And then, whole days typing it all into a spreadsheet.

1 · The night

You scan at the booth. At night, you go out.

Card, photo, price and minimum order stay together on the spot, not back at the hotel.

2 · Who had the lamps?

You liked it, you have the photo, and you don't even know the price.

That supplier is money left at the fair. With FairScan the photo, the card and the price are tied together the moment you take them.

3 · Compare without guessing

Forty suppliers, side by side.

Back home, you compare with price and MOQ in view, and you request the proforma when you've decided, not when you remember.

How it works

Two photos per product. FairScan does the rest.

01

One photo of the product

The app opens on the camera. The AI recognizes the product, describes it and files it under the booth you're at.

02

One photo of the card

It reads the supplier's name, WeChat, email and phone and links them to all their products. Cards are free, always.

03

Mark, build, send

Favorites, quantities and voice notes. The order is built per supplier and goes out as a proforma by WhatsApp or email, or as an Excel with photos.
